FAQ

Questions worth asking before you start.

Where should we start if our current website is a mess?

Start with the outcome: more enquiries, stronger credibility, or less time wasted fixing online problems. We can check what exists and tell you whether to improve it or rebuild.

Do we need to use all your services?

No. You can start with a website, hosting, search help, support or simple follow-up. The advantage is that each piece can work together later.

Will you write the website copy?

Yes. We can shape the pages and wording around your services, customers and offer. Good content is part of the build, not a decoration added at the end.

Can you take over hosting and business email?

Yes. We can move the website and email, secure the site, check backups and make sure the important settings are in place.
