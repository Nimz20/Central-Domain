Service FAQ

Good things to clarify early.

Can you move our existing email?

Yes. We can help plan the migration, set up mailboxes, update records and test device access after the move.

Will there be downtime?

We plan DNS and mailbox changes carefully to reduce disruption. Some propagation time is normal, but the move is managed around that.

Do you handle DNS records?

Yes. We can configure the records needed for hosting, email, SSL, verification and basic delivery hygiene.
