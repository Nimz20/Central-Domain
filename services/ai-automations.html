Service FAQ

Good things to clarify early.

Where should automation start?

Start with one repetitive workflow that costs time or causes errors, such as enquiry routing, document handling or follow-up preparation.

Will AI replace staff decisions?

No. We prefer automations that prepare, route and summarise work while keeping important decisions with people.

Can you connect our existing tools?

Usually, yes. We first check the tools, available integrations, data access and privacy requirements before proposing the workflow.

Show us the repetitive work.

Send the workflow, spreadsheet, inbox or admin process. We will tell you what can realistically be automated.
